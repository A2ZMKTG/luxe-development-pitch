The Method

Five moves to the top of the page

01
Foundation

A technically perfect, AI-readable site

Google and AI search can only rank what they can read and trust. We ship the technical layer your competitors skipped.

  • Full schema: LocalBusiness, Service, Project, FAQ, Review
  • Static, fast, crawlable build (not JavaScript-hidden)
  • llms.txt + clean structure for ChatGPT / Perplexity / Google AI
  • Core Web Vitals tuned for mobile
02
Architecture

A page for every money keyword

You can't rank for a search you don't have a page for. We build dedicated, deep pages for each high-value service — weighted to where the volume actually is.

  • First-class kitchen, bathroom, addition & basement pages
  • Custom-home & whole-home renovation pillars
  • Each page intent-matched, schema-marked and internally linked
03
Local

Own the western suburbs, town by town

"Near me" and "[service] in [town]" is how local buyers search. We build a real page for each priority suburb — and align your Google Business Profile to match.

  • 10–15 genuine suburb pages (Hinsdale, Oak Brook, Naperville…)
  • GBP optimization + the top-30 citation stack
  • No thin doorway pages — real, useful local content
04
Authority

A content & project engine that compounds

The highest-intent, lowest-competition searches are about cost and proof. We feed them — and turn every finished job into a ranking asset.

  • Cost guides ("kitchen remodel cost," 1,000/mo, low competition)
  • One indexable case-study page per completed project
  • Process & decision content that earns trust and links
05
Conversion

Rankings that become booked jobs

#1 only matters if it fills your calendar. Every page is built to convert the high-ticket buyer who lands on it.

  • Lead capture + click-to-call on every page
  • GHL CRM: instant lead alerts, missed-call text-back, review engine
  • Tracking (GA4 + Search Console) so we optimize to booked consultations
What To Expect

SEO compounds. Here's the arc.

Weeks 1–6

Build & Launch

New site live with full technical SEO, schema, core service + location pages, GBP + tracking.

Months 2–3

Index & Climb

Google indexes the new architecture; long-tail and local terms start ranking. Content engine begins.

Months 3–6

Momentum

Money keywords move up the page as authority, reviews and project pages accumulate.

Months 6–12

Dominance

Top-of-page for priority terms; the site becomes a consistent, compounding lead source.

SEO is a compounding asset, not an overnight switch — the work done in month one keeps paying in month twelve. Paid ads can run alongside to drive leads from week one while organic builds.

Straight talk: nobody can guarantee a specific ranking — anyone who does is selling you something. What we guarantee are the deliverables: a technically superior, conversion-built site and the ongoing work that earns rankings. In a market where the leaders left this much on the table, that's what wins.
