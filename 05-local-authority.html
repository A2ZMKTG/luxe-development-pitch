Who We Are

A Lemont builder with a standard you can see across the western suburbs.

Luxe Development was founded on one stubborn idea: the family hiring you to build their forever home shouldn't have to manage three companies to get it.

So we keep it all under one roof — architecture, engineering, interior design and construction, on one team and one contract. There's no handoff between an architect who drew it and a contractor who's never met them, no finger-pointing when something doesn't line up. The people who design your home are the people who build it, and they answer to you.

For more than two decades we've built ground-up custom homes and reimagined existing ones across Lemont, Hinsdale, Oak Brook, Burr Ridge and Naperville. We know these neighborhoods — the lots, the village permit desks, the architectural review boards — and we build to a standard meant to outlast trends and the people who set them.

Questions

Good to know

Where does Luxe Development build?

Across Chicago's western and southwest suburbs — Lemont, Hinsdale, Oak Brook, Burr Ridge, Naperville, Downers Grove, Western Springs and 20+ surrounding communities in DuPage, Cook and Will County.

What does "design-build" mean for me?

One company owns the architecture, engineering and construction. You sign one contract and hold one team accountable — instead of refereeing between a separate architect, engineer and general contractor.

Do you handle both custom homes and renovations?

Yes. We build ground-up custom homes and take on whole-home renovations, additions, and luxury kitchen, bath and basement projects.

How do you handle budget and timeline?

Selections and scope are locked into an itemized budget before construction begins, so the number you approve is the number we build to — with a clear schedule and milestone walk-throughs.

How do we get started?

With a complimentary site walkthrough. We'll review your property, discuss your vision, and outline a realistic path, timeline and budget range before you commit to anything.
