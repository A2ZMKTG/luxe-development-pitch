Who We Are

A Lemont builder, held to a standard you can see.

Luxe Development was founded on one idea: the family building their forever home shouldn't have to manage three companies to get it.

So we keep architecture, engineering, interior design and construction under one roof — one team, one contract, one standard. The people who design your home are the people who build it, and they answer to you.

For more than twenty years we've built ground-up custom homes and reimagined existing ones across Lemont, Hinsdale, Oak Brook, Burr Ridge and Naperville — to a standard meant to outlast the trends, and the people who set them.

Our Process

The Luxe Method

Four stages, one team, zero handoffs — the same path on a new kitchen as on a new home.

01

Vision & Architecture

We listen, walk the site, and let design and engineering define a realistic build before a dollar is committed.

02

Bespoke Curation

Finishes, fixtures and budget are selected and locked early — so the number you approve is the number we hold.

03

Master Construction

Our own crews build it, supervised on site daily, with milestone walk-throughs that keep you ahead of every decision.

04

White-Glove Handover

A detailed final walk-through, a punch list closed to zero, and a home delivered exactly as drawn.
