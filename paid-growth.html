When It Makes Sense

Paid Growth is the accelerator.

Organic SEO builds durable, compound rankings. Paid Growth fills the gap while it builds — and accelerates when you need to push volume fast.

You need leads now

A new website takes 6–12 months to reach top rankings organically. Paid ads can drive qualified calls in week one. Run both: ads fill the pipeline, SEO builds the foundation.

You want to dominate a specific suburb

Targeting Hinsdale specifically? A geo-targeted Google Search campaign puts Luxe Development at the top of every relevant search in that zip code — regardless of where organic rankings are.

You have a seasonal push

Spring remodel season. Post-summer "before school starts" rush. Pre-holiday interior makeovers. Paid Growth lets you scale up ad spend during high-intent windows and pull back after.

You're scaling up job volume

Growing from 3 active jobs to 6? Or launching into a new market (Burr Ridge, Oak Brook)? Paid Growth gives you the lead volume to support the growth before organic has caught up.
