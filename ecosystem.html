Citations

AI Website Chatbot

A GoHighLevel-powered chatbot on your site that greets visitors, answers common questions, qualifies intent, and collects contact info — even when you're on a job site. First response under 30 seconds.

Automated post-project review requests via SMS and email through GHL. Luxury buyers research harder than anyone — a 5.0 profile with 40+ reviews closes more consultations than any ad creative.

Capture Every Lead

AI Voice Receptionist

A GHL AI Voice agent answers every call 24/7, introduces itself as Luxe Development, qualifies the lead ("Is this for a new build or renovation?"), collects contact info, and books a consultation — even at 9pm on a Saturday.

Missed-Call Text-Back

The moment a call is missed, GHL fires an automatic text: "Hi, this is Luxe Development — sorry we missed you. Are you looking to discuss a new build or renovation? We'd love to connect." Simple, instant, effective.

Without it: A missed call gets no follow-up. The prospect Googles the next builder and calls them instead.

Luxury buyers don't sign in 48 hours. A GHL email + SMS sequence keeps Luxe Development top of mind over weeks — sharing project photos, the Luxe Method story, and a warm ask for a consultation — until they're ready to move.

Without it: Leads who aren't ready today go cold. 6 weeks later they've chosen a competitor who stayed in touch.

The Real Difference

One strand. Or the whole web.

A website alone answers one question: "Is this a real company?" The full ecosystem answers the five questions that actually drive revenue.

    Does it get you found? Only if you already rank — which takes 6–12 months of SEO work after launch, none of which happens automatically.
  • ✕
    Does it answer the phone? No. If Tom's on a job site, the call goes to voicemail. 40%+ of inbound calls in contracting go unanswered.
  • ✕
    Does it follow up on missed leads? No. A website is static. It has no idea a lead came and left.
  • ✕
    Does it generate reviews? No. Happy clients get a form and forget. Your 14 reviews stay at 14.

    Does it prove ROI? No. You'll never know which strand drove which job, what your cost-per-lead is, or what to scale.

  • ✓
    Automated follow-up: GHL nurture sequences chase warm leads for 60+ days with photos, stories, and soft CTAs — so the buyer signs when they're ready, not only when they happen to call.
  • ✓

    Monthly reporting: You see exactly where every lead came from, what it cost, and what it turned into. Marketing becomes accountable — and scalable.
