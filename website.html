The Architecture

Why this ranks when others don't.

Most contractor websites are one page about the company and a contact form. Google has nothing to index. An A2Z build gives Google 35–90 pages of targeted, relevant content.

01

Location Pages

A page for each suburb you serve — Hinsdale, Oak Brook, Burr Ridge — targeting "luxury home builder [city]" searches. Each page is unique content, not duplicated boilerplate.

02

Content That Answers Searches

Cost-guide pages ("How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Naperville?") capture buyers in research mode — before they've even decided on a contractor — and convert them at the highest rate.

03

Schema + AI Readiness

Structured data tells Google exactly what you do and where. The llms.txt file tells AI tools the same. When a prospect asks ChatGPT "who builds custom homes in Hinsdale?" — you're findable.
